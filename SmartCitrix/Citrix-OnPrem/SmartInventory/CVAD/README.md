# CVAD Inventory

Citrix Virtual Apps and Desktops on-premises inventory scripts.

## Scripts

- `SmartCitrix-OnPrem-CVADSite-Inventory.ps1`: exports site, controller, zone, admin, scope, role, and service status data.
- `SmartCitrix-OnPrem-CVADDelivery-Inventory.ps1`: exports catalogs, delivery groups, applications, application groups, access policies, entitlement policies, assignment policies, tags, and reboot schedules.
- `SmartCitrix-OnPrem-CVADMachineSessionHealth-Inventory.ps1`: exports machines, sessions, desktops, and health summaries.
- `SmartCitrix-OnPrem-CVADPolicy-Inventory.ps1`: exports Citrix policy sets, policies, settings, filters, and policy test status when supported by the local SDK.
- `SmartCitrix-OnPrem-CVADHostingPower-Inventory.ps1`: exports hosting connections, connection status, power schemes, power actions, delayed actions, catalog reboot schedules, and reboot cycles.
