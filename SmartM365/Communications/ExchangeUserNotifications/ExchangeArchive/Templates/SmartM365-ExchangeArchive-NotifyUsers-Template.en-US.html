ENABLING YOUR ARCHIVE MAILBOX
Important information

Hello {{UserName}},

From {{Date}}, an archive mailbox will be enabled on your email account.

Why?
To prepare the migration to Exchange Online and ensure a smooth transition.

What does this change for you?
  • Nothing will be lost.
  • A section Archive - {{UserName}} will appear in Outlook (left pane).
  • Your oldest emails may be moved there automatically to reduce the size of your primary mailbox.
  • Archived emails remain accessible and searchable at any time.
Action required?

No mandatory action is required from you.

How to use the archive?
  • Move large folders to the archive (right-click > Move).
  • Automatic archiving will progressively move older emails.
  • Outlook search includes the archive.
  • If needed, use the “All Outlook Items” scope to search both the primary mailbox and the archive.

Archive yourself to free up space

If you want to quickly reduce the size of your primary mailbox, you can manually archive certain emails or folders.

Method 1 – Move an entire folder (recommended)

The fastest option if you have many emails.

  1. Right-click a folder (e.g., “Projects 2022”)
  2. Click Move Folder
  3. Select Archive - {{UserName}}
  4. Confirm

All emails in the folder are moved in a single operation.

Method 2 – Archive multiple emails

  1. Select multiple emails
  2. Right-click
  3. Click Move
  4. Select a folder in the archive

Avoid moving emails one by one if the volume is significant.

Method 3 – Free up space quickly

To identify large emails:

  • Click Sort by
  • Select Size
  • Archive or delete the largest emails

Consider saving important attachments to OneDrive before deleting.

Don’t forget: regularly empty Deleted Items and Junk Email — these folders count toward your total mailbox size.
